Your Career Roadmap

You can begin exploring a career while you are still in school. Take it one step at a time.

01

Discover

Explore different fields and identify what interests you.

02

Learn

Study the basic knowledge and skills used in your chosen field.

03

Practise

Complete small projects to apply what you have learned.

04

Show Your Work

Organise your projects into a portfolio and keep improving.
